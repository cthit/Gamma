Your accepted clients

Here are the clients that you have agreed to share your data from Gamma with. You have the right to retract that approval at any time. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact ita@chalmers.it.

Retracting approval stops further access through that approval and asks Gamma's admins to follow up on erasure at official clients. It does not erase copies a client already holds. Erasure, where applicable, must happen without undue delay. Reapproving a client does not cancel an outstanding erasure request.

For official clients, Gamma keeps your CID, user ID, client name and ID, reason and request dates until admins resolve the request. On clearance it removes your CID and the client name, and keeps the remaining identifiers, dates and clearing admin for 365 days to track the handling of your request. These records are personal data. Read the privacy information.
