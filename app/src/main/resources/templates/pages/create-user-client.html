Create new client

This client will be created in your name, and will be shown everytime a user attempts to authenticate with gamma to your client.

Your email is shown to users who are asked to approve your client, so that they can ask you questions, and to users who have approved it, on the page for deleting their account, so that they can ask you to delete the data your client has about them. It is only shown to users who are signed in to Gamma, never to anyone who is not.
