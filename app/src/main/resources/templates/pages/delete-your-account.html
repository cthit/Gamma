Deleting your account

Deleting your account can't be reversed.

Gamma removes your profile, owned clients, approvals and credentials when deletion succeeds. Uploaded avatars are queued for file removal, with retries if storage is unavailable. Read the privacy information for retained request records and contact details. You can contact ita@chalmers.it after your account is deleted.

Official clients you have approved

When you delete your account, Gamma's administrators follow up on erasure with these clients. Erasure, where applicable, must happen without undue delay. Admins must respond to your request within one month.

What Gamma keeps after you delete your account

Until a client above has deleted your data, Gamma keeps your user id, your cid and the name of the client. After an admin confirms erasure, Gamma keeps a record of that confirmation for 365 days. The record has your user id, the id of the client, why your data had to be deleted and the dates of what happened. It also identifies the clearing admin. It doesn't have your cid or the name of the client. These identifiers are still personal data.

You have retracted your approval of official clients before, and admins have recorded that those erasure requests were completed. Gamma keeps a record that your data was deleted for each of them, for one year after it was confirmed. The record has your user id, the id of the client, why your data had to be deleted and the dates of what happened.

Clients made by other users

These clients are not official, so Gamma can't make sure they delete your data. If you want them to, email their owners before you delete your account.

Gamma cannot delete the data stored in your applications, exports or backups for you. Delete all personal information you collected about users through these clients before continuing. If you must retain particular data by law, contact ita@chalmers.it to resolve that before deleting your account.
