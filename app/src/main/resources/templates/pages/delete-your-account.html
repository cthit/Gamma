Deleting your account

Deleting your account can't be reversed.

Official clients you have approved

Gamma's administrators will make sure that these clients delete the data they have about you. Until they have confirmed that for a client, Gamma keeps your user id, your cid and the name of that client. The deadline for this is one month after you delete your account. When they have confirmed it, your cid and the client name are removed. Your user id and the id of the client are then kept for one year, as a record that your data was deleted.

Clients made by other users

These clients are not official, so Gamma can't make sure they delete your data. If you want them to, email their owners before you delete your account.
