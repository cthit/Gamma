Sign in and security

You are an admin

Admins sign in with a passkey. They can't reset how they sign in by email or with a recovery key. If you lose all your passkeys, ask another admin to reset how you sign in.

Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your phone, a security key or your computer's fingerprint reader or PIN, without a password. It is the safest way to sign in.

Make signing in with your password safer by also asking for a code from an authenticator app, such as the ones built into iOS and many password managers.

Email recovery

If you forget your password or lose your passkeys, you can set up a new way to sign in through a link mailed to you. Anyone who can read your email can do that too.

You can turn email recovery off. You then get a recovery key to get back in with instead, which you must keep safe. If you lose it too, only an admin can let you back in.
