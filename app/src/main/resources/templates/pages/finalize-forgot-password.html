Sign in with a passkey (recommended)

Instead of a new password, add a passkey: your phone, a security key or your computer's fingerprint reader or PIN. No password to remember, and it can't be phished.

Starting recovery removes your old passkeys and password and revokes your previous Gamma sessions.

Resetting your password removes old passkeys and revokes your previous Gamma sessions. If you use an authenticator app, it is still required.
