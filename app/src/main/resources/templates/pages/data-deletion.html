Data deletion requests

A user has deleted their account, or retracted their approval of an official client. Each request must be handled within one month, as required by the GDPR. Make sure the official client has deleted the data it has about the user, then clear the request. Clearing it removes the cid and the client name. The ids are kept for one year as a record that the data was deleted.
