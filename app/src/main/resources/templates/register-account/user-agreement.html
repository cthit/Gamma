This agreement refers to IT:s user account system Gamma.

IT will collect and manage name, nickname, year of admission, CID, phone number, e-mail, commitées and societies you are and have been part of, preferred language and your profile picture. The data is managed in order to authenticate you to IT and third party services. Furthermore, the data is used to create a profile that connects to IT services as well as any third party services you approve. The data is also used if we need to contact you for matters of special interest to you. The data is also handled for statistical purposes.

Your account profile is saved until you delete it or IT decides to delete it. If IT decides to delete your account, you will be notified at least 30 days before deletion. The request records described below can remain after profile deletion, including when an admin deletes the account.

Withdrawal and erasure requests

Withdrawing a client's approval stops further access through that approval. It does not erase data already received by the client. Gamma records an erasure follow-up request for official clients when you withdraw approval or your account is deleted. Applicable erasure must take place without undue delay. We must respond to a rights request within one month; contact us about any exception, delay or outcome.

To handle these requests and record how they were resolved, Gamma retains your CID and user ID, the official client's name and ID, the reason and the request dates until admins resolve the request. Open requests do not expire automatically: overdue requests require follow-up, not silent deletion. When an admin confirms erasure, Gamma removes the CID and client name. It retains the user and client IDs, reason, dates and clearing admin for 365 days after clearance, then removes that record in the daily cleanup. These records remain personal data and are available only to admins. They are used for rights handling and accountability, rather than relying on the withdrawn approval.

For clients made by other users, you can contact the owner to request erasure. Gamma shows the owner's name and email for that purpose. A withdrawn-client contact reminder stores only a link between your account and the client. It is removed when you dismiss it, reapprove the client, or your account or the client is deleted. Save any contact information you need before deleting your account. Gamma cannot verify erasure in independently operated applications.

Account deletion removes Gamma's stored OAuth grants and sessions. Avatar files are queued for deletion with retries; a periodic sweep also removes older unreferenced uploads. Clients may have their own sessions and copies of previously received data. Contact us for help exercising your rights, for the outcome of a request, or for information about retained data, backups and safeguards.

Name, Chalmers ID, nickname and year of admission is shared with all users of Gamma. Data may also be shared with third party services with the user’s approval.
